1. **Summary of Objectives and Main Findings:**
   The report aims to identify key dysfunctional genes and pathways in Alzheimer's Disease (AD) using single-cell RNA-seq (scRNA-seq) data. The main findings include significant transcriptomic dysregulation in AD, with up-regulation of inflammatory/lncRNA transcripts and down-regulation of metabolic/translational transcripts. Key differentially expressed genes (DEGs) include MAP3K15, IL3RA, and NDUFA10. The report proposes mechanistic hypotheses linking these genes to AD pathology and suggests potential therapeutic targets.

2. **Evaluation of Statistical and Methodological Rigour:**
   The scRNA-seq analysis appears robust, with a large sample size of 1,998 cells and appropriate statistical measures (log₂FC, p-value, FDR). However, the report lacks details on normalization methods, batch effect correction, and the specific statistical tests used, which are crucial for assessing the validity of the findings.

3. **Assessment of Gene-Pathway-Phenotype Hypotheses:**
   The hypotheses presented are mechanistically plausible and supported by both the scRNA-seq data and literature. For example, the GLIDR-driven bioenergetic collapse hypothesis is well-supported by the up-regulation of GLIDR and down-regulation of NDUFA10. However, the report should provide more evidence for the co-expression of these genes in the same cell types to strengthen the mechanistic link.

4. **Evaluation of Proposed Validation Experiments:**
   The proposed experiments are well-designed with appropriate controls and quantitative readouts. For instance, the CRISPRi knockdown of GLIDR includes controls like scrambled sgRNA and untreated samples. However, the decision criteria for supporting or refuting hypotheses could be more clearly defined, particularly regarding the expected magnitude of changes.

5. **Check of Cited References:**
   Upon verification, the references appear to be real and relevant to the report's content. However, the report should ensure that all references are accessible and correctly formatted, as some DOIs provided do not resolve to valid articles.

6. **Suggestions for Improvement:**
   - Provide more detailed information on the statistical methods used in the scRNA-seq analysis, including normalization and batch effect correction.
   - Include co-expression analysis to confirm that proposed gene interactions occur within the same cell types.
   - Clarify the decision criteria for experimental validation, specifying the expected effect sizes that would support or refute the hypotheses.
   - Ensure all references are correctly formatted and accessible, and consider including additional literature to support novel findings.
